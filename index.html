Detectability Transition in Network Alignment

Bachelor's thesis, URV · Sep 2024 – Jun 2025 · Best Bachelor's Thesis

When can the true correspondence between two noisy observations of the same network be recovered from their structure alone? Supervised by Roger Guimerà.

Details
  • Built a Bayesian inference framework using MCMC with parallel tempering to sample the posterior over network alignments.
  • Identified transitions between algorithmic failure and theoretical impossibility of recovery, including regimes where the true alignment is detectable in principle but computationally inaccessible.
  • Analysed these phenomena through statistical physics, studying the posterior landscape and how low-energy configurations relate to the ground truth.
  • Bayesian Inference
  • MCMC
  • Parallel Tempering
  • Statistical Physics
